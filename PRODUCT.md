# CoSec Workspace

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Owner selected Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind, MySQL InnoDB, Drizzle and Zod. Local milestone followed by one shared server.

## Users and purpose
Internal Malaysian company secretarial staff managing an initial 10–20 private companies limited by shares. Preparers draft resolutions, colleagues optionally review them, administrators manage templates and accounts. Since 1 November 2026 the build focuses on resolution preparation; the job, filing, billing, share-register and beneficial-ownership features were removed.

## Operating context
Owner-authorised provisional defaults precede a team walkthrough. Manual practice and a possible Excel tracker have not been inspected. Runtime works offline on a running local host; signatures, client messages and submissions occur externally. Fictional data only for evaluation.

## Constraints
DESIGN.md Option A is approved. The full DESIGN_SPEC.md and original revision 2 brief retain W01–W12 scope. Separate document and record states, immutable generated outputs, company scopes, attributed audit and version reviews are mandatory; job, filing and billing states return only with those features. No approved legal catalogue exists. Professional validation, actual templates, retention and shared hosting remain open decisions.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; desktop only (no phone or tablet layouts); keyboard, larger text and reduced motion. Concise visible labels and material feedback without routine helper prose.
