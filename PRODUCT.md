# CoSec Workspace

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Owner selected Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind, MySQL InnoDB, Drizzle and Zod. Local milestone followed by one shared server.

## Users and purpose
Internal Malaysian company secretarial staff managing an initial 10–20 private companies limited by shares. Executives prepare, reviewers decide exact versions, authorised lodgers record external filings, administrators manage accounts. The local build supports annual returns, director changes, members/share capital/transfers, restricted BO assessments and integrated reminders.

## Operating context
Owner-authorised provisional defaults precede a team walkthrough. Manual practice and a possible Excel tracker have not been inspected. Runtime works offline on a running local host; signatures, client messages and submissions occur externally. Fictional data only for evaluation.

## Constraints
DESIGN.md Option A is approved. The full DESIGN_SPEC.md and original revision 2 brief retain W01–W12 scope. Separate job, document, record, filing and billing states, immutable historical snapshots, company scopes, attributed audit and version reviews are mandatory. No approved legal catalogue exists. Professional validation, actual templates, retention and shared hosting remain open decisions.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; responsive work at 390px, 768px and desktop; keyboard, larger text and reduced motion. Concise visible labels and material feedback without routine helper prose.
